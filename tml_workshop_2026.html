Overview

The AI community is advancing at an unprecedented pace, making timely and effective communication increasingly important for exchanging ideas, fostering collaborations, and conducting impactful research. This post-NeurIPS workshop on Trustworthy Machine Learning aims to bring together leading researchers around the world to share recent advances, discuss emerging challenges, and strengthen connections within the community.

The workshop will showcase a diverse range of recent research, with three main objectives: (1) exchanging new ideas and perspectives on trustworthy machine learning and related areas; (2) inspiring junior researchers and students to pursue ambitious and impactful research; and (3) fostering new collaborations and strengthening the research community.
